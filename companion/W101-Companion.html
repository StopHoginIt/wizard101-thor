Good to know

Labels are by position: A = bottom, B = right, X = left, Y = top. If your Thor swaps A/B in its settings, the game follows the swap.

The cursor slows near the stick's centre: small pushes for cards, full push to cross the screen.

GameNative's own menu: press the Thor's Back gesture/key.
