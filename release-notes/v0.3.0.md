Bottom-screen shortcut buttons.

- **W101 Panel** (new Android app, `W101Panel.apk`): a strip of Wizard101 buttons on the Thor's bottom screen (Backpack, Spells, Quests, Map, Potion, Pet, Mark, Recall, Home, Friends, Chat, Esc) on top of GameNative's bottom-screen touchpad. The controller keeps working after tapping a button. See `panel/README.md`.
- **W101Quick 1.7**: while the game runs, listens on `127.0.0.1:47101` (on the Thor only) and presses the matching keys for the panel. Put `no-panel.txt` next to `W101Quick.exe` to turn it off.
- CI also publishes a `nightly` pre-release with test builds; `laptop\Install-Panel.cmd` installs the panel app from it over USB.
