Card helper reworked, then parked; faster launcher clicks.

- **W101 Panel 0.24**: battles keep the shortcut tiles by default. The card helper (your hand on the bottom screen) was rebuilt for speed in 0.20-0.23 (screen read about 15 times a second, hovered cards and enchant/target prompts recognised, steady layout, live battle picture, swipe down to discard), but a copy of the cards always trails the game a little, so it's switched off. The 🃏 Cards button in the panel's top bar turns it back on if you want to try it. Battle tracking (pips in the top bar, no stick right-clicks during fights) works either way.
- **W101Quick 2.5**: clicks from the panel take about 0.2 s instead of 0.7 s and no longer queue behind each other; timings can be tuned in `click.txt` (see launcher/README.md).
- Install: W101Panel.apk from this release over the old one; W101Quick-arm64.exe as W101Quick.exe next to the game (restart the game).
