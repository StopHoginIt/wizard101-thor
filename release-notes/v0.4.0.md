Card helper and a Wizard101-styled bottom screen.

- **W101 Panel 0.8**: restyled after Wizard101's UI. In battle, your hand appears on the bottom screen: tap a card to cast, hold to discard, PASS / DRAW / FLEE, then tap who the spell is for on a still picture of the battle (skipped for spells with no target). The app reads the hand from the top screen with Android's screen-capture permission; nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
- **W101Quick 1.8**: clicks from the panel no longer leave the mouse pointer on your cards (a hovered card grows and covered its neighbours).
- Docs: how the panel works (`panel/README.md`) and why capture happens on the Android side (`docs/FINDINGS.md`).
